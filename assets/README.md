# Ilustración del lago

`lake-frame.png` fue generada con la herramienta integrada OpenAI imagegen el 13 de octubre de 2026 para implementar el concepto visual aprobado. Es una ilustración de paisaje, orillas y sedimentos con un área de agua transparente. No contiene datos de la simulación.

La aplicación coloca las capas calculadas detrás del marco, añade organismos, mezcla, ciclo de sol/luna, regla de profundidad y etiquetas. No necesita una API de generación de imágenes durante su ejecución. El archivo original se conserva sin modificaciones; el recorte de cielo se realiza al dibujar en canvas.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset: wide landscape illustrated frame for a LIVE lake thermocline scientific cross-section canvas. Generate ONLY scenic art, never UI or text. Aspect ratio about 3:1 wide panorama. At top 25%: beautiful natural lake shoreline panorama, pale blue sky with small clouds, distant blue rolling hills, forest of pines and deciduous trees, late morning sun, detailed realistic painted landscape like a natural history museum illustration. A perfectly horizontal water surface spans from x=7% to x=93% at y=25%. Below that surface: show an exposed rocky and earth lake basin cutaway ONLY AROUND THE EDGES: sloped detailed earthy rock banks on left and right tapering from x=7% at top to x=16% at bottom (left), from x=93% to x=84% on right. Rocky sediment floor runs along y=94%-100%. The broad WATER INTERIOR must be fully TRANSPARENT, an empty transparent opening from y=25% to y=94% following basin slopes. This is essential: do NOT paint water into the interior opening; a separate program will paint live water data behind this transparent frame. No fish, no underwater vegetation in opening, no underwater light rays, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no icons, no lines, no typography, no app controls. The sky, forest and rocky earth frame opaque; entire central lake water region transparent alpha, not checkerboard painted in. Detailed realistic but softly illustrated mountains and rocky forest shoreline, restrained depth textures, calm inviting scientific aesthetic. Edge geometry simple enough for a scientific overlay, no perspective distorted water plane; frontal scientific cutaway with horizontal depth layers to be filled separately. No aquarium, no tank, no fantasy.
